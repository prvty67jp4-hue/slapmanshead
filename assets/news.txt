# Main menu redesign (Nov 2026)
- Your fighter now stands on the map behind the menu, framed by a slow camera
- One big PLAY button opens every way to play: Quick Play, lobbies, bots, Gauntlet, Training
- Your Steam name is used automatically (change it in Settings > Game)
- Loadout: one gun grid, two slots. Click a slot, then a gun to fill it

# Look and feel
- New fonts, menus and HUD
- Weapon cards with stats on the loadout screen
- Full controller support in every menu
