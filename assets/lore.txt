[world]
title = Varrow and the Rush
intro = Varrow is a walled city of black stone and lantern light, built on the ruins of an older one. Its courts were burned in the Ash Riots a generation ago, and nothing replaced them but the Rush: sanctioned duels fought in the old fortress courtyards, steel in one hand and powder in the other. Debts, land, pardons, inheritances, grudges - in Varrow they are all settled the same way.
court = The Rush is run by the Ashen Court, five masked judges no one has seen unmasked. They set the bouts, keep the ledgers and pay the winners in Writs.
writs = A Writ is a sealed parchment stamped in grey wax. One Writ can wipe a debt, free a prisoner or hand over a house. Ten can buy a pardon for murder. Everyone in the Rush is fighting for Writs, and everyone is fighting for something different.
rush = Fighters call it "rushing": the moment the bell rings and two people who have nothing left to lose run straight at each other across the stones.

[map.sunfall_keep]
name = Sunfall Keep
lore = The Ashen Court's official duelling ground, a sandstone keep where the bell rings at sunset. Bouts fought here are recorded in the great ledger, and the stands are always full.

[map.undercroft]
name = The Undercroft
lore = The cellars and cisterns beneath Varrow, where the illegal fights happen. No judges, no ledgers, no Writs - just the money in the room and whoever is still standing.

[map.old_marble_court]
name = Old Marble Court
lore = The white courtyard where the very first Rush was fought after the Ash Riots. The marble is cracked and the banners are faded, but every fighter wants to win here at least once.

[map.bridgeworks]
name = Varrow Bridgeworks
lore = The old workshops and stone bridges over the river Varr, where the Foundry Guild once moved iron across the city. Now crews fight over it, team against team, for the right to the river toll.
