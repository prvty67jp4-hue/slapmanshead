assets/env/props/varrow_*.glb and varrow_props.png (Varrow Bridgeworks: bridge railings, the archway,
  the dockside crane, stacked crates and the wall lanterns)
  Original work made for this game with tools/blender/build_props.py (no third-party parts).
Varrow Bridgeworks' dusk sky and its river water are drawn by the game's own code (src/env.rs,
  src/gpu.rs); the map's stone and wood reuse the Poly Haven CC0 textures listed above.

Executions (src/exec.rs): every move, name, camera shot and announcer line is this game's own.
  The animation uses the Quaternius "Universal Animation Library" clips listed above (sword, hook,
  throw, jump, chop and kneel clips), cut and retimed, with hand-keyed poses on top. The execution
  sounds (whoosh, impact, ring-out and crowd) are designed in code (src/audio.rs, design_exec) from
  the CC0 recordings above (swing, dash, flesh, boom, clang and wall sounds) plus synthesised
  layers. The announcer lines are in assets/lore.txt, [executions].

Tactics pass (23 November 2026): no new downloaded files. The new sounds are made in the game from the CC0
clips above: metal and water footsteps, the water splash, indoor and outdoor gunshot tails, the kill
confirm, the smoke and flash pops and the ping chime. The utility names (Ash Lantern, Magnesium Flare,
Forge Smoke, Spark Cracker), the callout names and the clock tower, cranes and forges are original.
